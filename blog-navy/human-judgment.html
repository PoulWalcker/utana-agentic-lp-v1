Agents prepare, people decide

UTANA / FIELD NOTES

Keep people in the loop: agents prepare the work, while accountable reviewers approve consequential actions before execution.

Separate preparation from decisions

LLMs can produce confident but incorrect answers or miss important context. Use them to gather information, compare options, and draft recommendations. Keep decisions that affect customers, employees, money, or commitments with an authorised person.

Require approval before action

Pause before sending an offer, publishing content, changing a financial record, or making a hiring decision. Show the reviewer the proposed action, supporting sources, and unresolved questions. Approval should apply to that specific action; changed details require a new review.

Make the approval enforceable

Keep preparation and execution separate. The execution step should check a recorded approval, with the reviewer, time, and approved version attached. A model’s own statement that an action is approved is not permission.

Escalate uncertainty and learn

Missing evidence, conflicting records, and requests outside the workflow’s scope should go to a person. Record corrections and failures, then update the workflow and test it before releasing changes.
